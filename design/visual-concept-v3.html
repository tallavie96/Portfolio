Color

Contrast is calculated live from the tokens. Rule: on pink, blue and butter bands, text is ink or plum only. Secondary grey text is for paper and cards.

Type

Display · Bricolage Grotesque 800, condensedTal Lavie
Soft display · Bricolage 400One family, loud or quiet
Body · IBM Plex Sans 17pxCase-study text stays near 65 characters per line, with comfortable line height for long reads.
Notes · Courier Primesticky notes, captions, dates, labels (2023–2026)

Bricolage Grotesque has a width axis, so one family covers the loud cropped headlines (Nest) and the soft headings (Birthday). Courier Prime brings the typewriter from the scrapbook. Note for later: Bricolage and Courier Prime have no Hebrew. When Hebrew resumes, each needs a Hebrew partner; I'll test pairs then.

How the mix is decided

  • Look and feel from v2: your references are warm, so the base is paper, plum and strong pastels.
  • Thinking layer from v1: frame labels, selection handles and the cursor show this is a product designer's board, not a party invite.
  • Restraint: one torn edge (hero into work), one redline, one cursor. Other bands meet with straight edges.

Where your layouts go

  • Three cards in a row (Explore styles): the homepage work grid. Clearer for recruiters than the staggered grid.
  • A · Split opener (Birthday): the top of every case study. Title, summary, role pills, one big image. The soft pink glow is the one gradient on the site.
  • B · Image + text rows (Nest): case-study chapters and AI playground entries. Stacks to one column on phones.
  • C · Mosaic wall (Project gallery): the AI playground overview and screen galleries inside case studies.

Interaction

  • Hover a project: the card lifts, straightens and gets selection handles, like picking up a frame.
  • Keyboard focus shows the same selection box, so keyboard users see the full effect.
  • Touch devices: no hover effects; cards work with a tap.
  • Reduced motion: the cursor appears in place, nothing moves.
